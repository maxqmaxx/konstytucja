# World constitutions: a workshop for thinking about a better (Polish) constitution

Open data and worked examples for anyone discussing constitutional design, with a focus on Poland:

- prevalence of 331 constitutional features (Comparative Constitutions Project topic coding) across 188 constitutions in force, also for constitutions adopted since 2000 and for the EU-27;
- the Polish Constitution of 1997, article by article, against that background, with manually reviewed gaps;
- 53 745 foreign provisions with a Polish one-line summary, normative strength and debate-problem tags (machine-generated, Claude Opus 5.5, closed vocabularies), each linked to the full text on Constitute;
- worked examples, 24 proposals for Poland, and the code to reproduce everything.

**Browse it online: [maxqmaxx.github.io/konstytucja](https://maxqmaxx.github.io/konstytucja/)** (Polish interface): the Polish Constitution article by article against the world; 23 debate problems leading to foreign solutions; 331 topic pages with filters (EU only, post-2000, normative strength, variant); exact variants for 23 key design choices (term limits, veto override, amendment, emergencies, constitutional court, judicial council, judge removal, immunity, central bank, referendum…); a constitution builder (pick your own set and see the closest constitutions, differences from Poland and known tensions); statistics on length and popularity of solutions; a world map by feature or year of adoption; side-by-side comparison of 2–3 constitutions; 24 proposals with risks and safeguards; an atlas of 130 unusual provisions; country pages; and a personal folder ("teczka") to collect provisions and export them to Word, Markdown or PDF with citations (stored only in your browser). Every record has a "report an error" link that opens a prefilled GitHub issue; light and dark theme.

Constitution texts and translations by third parties are not redistributed; see [docs/czego-nie-ma.md](docs/czego-nie-ma.md). Code: MIT. Own content: CC BY 4.0. Data as of 2026-11-04. Most documentation is in Polish.
